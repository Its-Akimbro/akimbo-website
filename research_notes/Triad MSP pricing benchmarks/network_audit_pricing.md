# Network Audit / IT Infrastructure Assessment Pricing (Piedmont Triad focus, with national benchmarks)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Method: web search and page fetches (about 16 tool calls). Note on evidence quality: almost every source is MSP or vendor marketing. Where a claim comes only from a search-result snippet rather than a page I fetched, it is marked "(snippet)". I found **no published dollar price for a network audit from any Triad-based firm**.

## 1. What do Triad-area MSPs / IT firms charge for network assessments, IT audits, security assessments? Are many offered free?

### Takeaway
No Triad firm I found publishes a price for an assessment. The visible ones offer it **free, as a sales tool**: a "free assessment" before a managed-IT quote, a free 30-minute call, or a free risk or cost-savings assessment. The price a Triad buyer sees for an audit is therefore $0 (or "call us"). A published fixed price would stand out, and Akimbo would have to justify it against "free".

## 2. National / US price ranges for small-business network audits, how they scale, and what scope limits sellers attach

### Takeaway
For a single-office SMB, national (non-Triad) published ranges cluster at **$1,500-$3,000 for under 25 endpoints**, **$3,000-$5,000 for 25-75 endpoints**, and **$5,000-$7,500 for multi-site setups with 75-200 endpoints**. Broader "network security assessment" ranges are $1,500-$6,000. Security audits with pentesting run $3,000-$15,000+. Freelancers start around $500. Sellers scale price by endpoint count and site count, and also by on-prem servers, compliance needs and pentest add-ons.

### Cited Findings
- **Sequentur** (multi-office MSP including Asheville NC; "honest market ranges as of 2026", no publication date): single office under 25 endpoints **$1,500-$3,000**; single office 25-75 endpoints **$3,000-$5,000**; multi-site 75-200 endpoints **$5,000-$7,500**; larger or specialized (HIPAA, CMMC, SOC 2, M&A diligence) **$7,500+**. Overall SMB range "$1,500 and $7,500 depending on scope" — [Sequentur](https://www.sequentur.com/what-is-a-network-assessment-and-why-your-business-should-have-one).
- **RIT Company** (Addison, IL / Chicagoland; Aug 12, 2026): network security assessment **$1,500-$6,000** typical SMB. Vulnerability assessment is given as $1,500-$6,000 in one table and $1,000-$5,000 in another (the page contradicts itself). Penetration test **$5,000-$30,000**. "MSP baseline security audit: often free". Cost drivers: users/endpoints/IPs, on-prem vs cloud vs hybrid, number of locations, compliance, pentest inclusion. Says the assessment gives the best value for 10-100 employee businesses — [RIT Company](https://ritcompany.com/blog/what-is-a-network-security-assessment-whats-included-checklist-and-how-often-your-business-needs/).
- **Fusion Computing** (Canada, prices in CAD): fixed-fee IT assessment "From $3,000" CAD. The entry price covers a small, single-tenant, cloud-only environment, about 3 days of work. Extra sites, on-prem servers or a second legal entity add assessment days at a published day rate. Fee stays fixed unless scope changes — [Fusion Computing](https://fusioncomputing.ca/it-business-assessment/).
- A small-business network security audit is estimated at **$3,000-$8,000 for a 10-50 user, single-location office**. Basic small-business network pentests run **$5,000-$15,000** (snippets, security vendors) — [7ASecurity 2026](https://7asecurity.com/blog/2025/12/network-pentest-costs-2026/), [Help Net Security 2025](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2025/07/16/estimating-security-audit-costs/).
- Basic vulnerability assessment for a small company is **~$3,000**; IT security audit assessment fee **$3,000-$10,000** depending on scope and assets (snippets; pentest vendors, biased upward) — [Qualysec](https://qualysec.com/it-security-audit-cost/), [Astra](https://www.getastra.com/blog/security-audit/it-security-audit-cost/).
- Onboarding plus network assessment as a one-time MSP fee: **$1,000-$5,000** — [Aalpha 2026](https://www.aalpha.net/blog/how-much-does-managed-it-services-cost/).
- Typical cybersecurity projects run $10,000-$49,999 at US provider rates of $150-$199/hr, with focused network or security assessments "at the lower end of that range—or below it" (snippet) — [Clutch MSP pricing](https://clutch.co/it-services/msp/pricing).
- MSP compliance assessment benchmarks: HIPAA $5,000-$15,000; CMMC Level 1 $5,000-$10,000 — [Scopable](https://scopable.io/blog/msp-compliance-pricing-guide).
- **Freelancer floor**: a Thumbtack provider says "the average pricing for a network audit and/or design starts at $500 or $75-$100 hour for infrastructure related work". Another bills $95/hr with a 2-hour minimum (snippets) — [Thumbtack Toledo](https://www.thumbtack.com/-Toledo-OH/service/2178512), [Thumbtack Miami](https://www.thumbtack.com/fl/miami/it-services/computer-repair-support-services).
- Hourly rates: network engineer / infrastructure specialist **$125-$200/hr**; small IT firms **$75-$125/hr**, larger firms $100-$225/hr (snippets, consultancy blogs) — [Consilien](https://consilien.com/news/it-consulting-cost), [IT Companies Network](https://itcompanies.net/blog/it-consulting-rates).
- High outlier: a single-location IT environment assessment at **$8,000-$30,000** (Orange County, CA SEO page; treat as an upper bound) — [BriteCity](https://britecity.com/resources/pseo/it-consulting-cost-small-business-irvine).

### Inferences
- An Akimbo-style scope (one site, a device cap of about 25-50 network/server devices, inventory plus risk report plus walk-through) fits the national **$1,500-$3,000** tier. At $125-$150/hr, that buys about 10-24 hours of work.
- Scope limits in the market are expressed as **endpoints per site** (25 / 75 / 200 breakpoints at Sequentur), **number of sites**, **on-prem servers**, and **assessment days** (Fusion: about 3 days at entry). Most sellers don't publish a hard device cap. Akimbo's "up to N devices, one site" is unusually explicit.
- Akimbo counts infrastructure devices (switches, APs, firewalls, servers, hypervisors), not user endpoints. Its device cap can therefore be much lower than a user-endpoint cap for the same size of office. Say so clearly to avoid confusing buyers.

### Gaps
- I found no r/msp practitioner threads (search didn't surface Reddit), and no Kaseya, ConnectWise or Service Leadership survey figure specifically for one-time assessment fees.
- I found no HomeGuide or Thumbtack page with a published average for "network audit".

## 3. What deliverables come with paid vs free assessments?

### Takeaway
Free assessments are typically a scoping conversation plus an automated high-level scan, followed by a sales proposal. Paid assessments deliver documented artefacts: a network diagram, a full device inventory with firmware and end-of-life data, a severity-ranked findings list, a 30/60/90-day or 12-24-month remediation roadmap, an executive summary and a documented baseline.

### Cited Findings
- Free or low-cost MSP assessments are usually "a scoping conversation plus a high-level scan, not a full engagement", and a different product from a paid assessment — [Sequentur](https://www.sequentur.com/what-is-a-network-assessment-and-why-your-business-should-have-one).
- Paid deliverables (Sequentur): current network diagram; complete device inventory (models, firmware, end-of-life dates); prioritized findings with severity ratings and fixes; 12-24 month remediation roadmap; documented baseline. Coverage: topology, performance, security, patch status, backup verification, configuration, documentation — [Sequentur](https://www.sequentur.com/what-is-a-network-assessment-and-why-your-business-should-have-one).
- Paid deliverables (RIT): asset inventory and scoping; vulnerability scanning with severity scores; firewall and configuration review; access control and privilege review; Wi-Fi and remote access assessment; risk-ranked report with 30/60/90-day plans and executive summary; pentest sold separately. A "free Network Security Audit" gives "a clear baseline" — [RIT Company](https://ritcompany.com/blog/what-is-a-network-security-assessment-whats-included-checklist-and-how-often-your-business-needs/).
- Paid deliverables (Fusion, about CAD $3,000+): executive summary; current-state registers (assets, identities, applications, vendors, contracts, renewals, single points of failure); ITIL 4 and CIS IG1 control positions; ranked remediation portfolio (do now / do next / decide / don't); transition scope pack — [Fusion Computing](https://fusioncomputing.ca/it-business-assessment/).
- Free, Triad-marketed: Petronella's free assessment ends in "a clear recommendation with pricing" (snippet), i.e. a sales quote — [Petronella Greensboro](https://petronellatech.com/managed-it-services-greensboro-nc/). Trinity's free offer is a 30-minute assessment (snippet) — [Clutch: Trinity](https://clutch.co/profile/trinity-solutions). Benchmark's free review walks through network, security posture and backups, then quotes a monthly fee (snippet) — [benchmarkns.com](https://benchmarkns.com/).

### Inferences
- Akimbo's planned deliverables (device inventory, plain-English risk report, walk-through, no-obligation fix quote) match the paid tier, and are more than the free tier gives. Positioning: "a real deliverable you keep, not a sales scan".

### Gaps
- I found no side-by-side sample reports comparing free and paid outputs from a single vendor.

## 4. Is it common to credit the audit fee against a later managed-services contract or project?

### Takeaway
I found weak evidence. The only explicit published policy I found says the fee is **not** credited. Many MSPs avoid the question by making the assessment free, or by folding it into a paid onboarding fee for new managed clients. I found no published example of a "credited toward" policy, though the practice is anecdotally common in sales.

### Cited Findings
- Fusion Computing: "The assessment fee is not credited against any resulting agreement", so it stands as paid, independent work — [Fusion Computing](https://fusioncomputing.ca/it-business-assessment/).
- Many MSPs "fold an initial assessment into onboarding for managed IT services" (Matrix MSP, snippet) — [Matrix MSP](https://matrixmspllc.com/feeds/blog/network-assessment).
- An onboarding fee "covers initial setup, network assessment, and getting all your systems under management". A one-time onboarding plus network assessment fee runs $1,000-$5,000 — [Aalpha](https://www.aalpha.net/blog/how-much-does-managed-it-services-cost/), [Innovative](https://blog.innovativeinc.net/blog/managed-it-services-cost).
- Petronella's pricing and blog pages say nothing about crediting — [Petronella Pricing](https://petronellatech.com/pricing/).

### Inferences
- A credit (for example, 100% of the audit fee credited against a fix project or the first months of managed service if signed within 30-60 days) would set Akimbo apart from the free-scan crowd while keeping the paid filter. This is a design option; I found no market data to support specific terms.

### Gaps
- I found no published seller that credits the audit fee and states its terms. Searches for "credited toward" or "applied toward" assessment-fee language returned only the contrary Fusion example.

## 5. How do Triad prices compare to Charlotte, Raleigh-Durham or national averages?

### Takeaway
I couldn't make a direct price comparison because no NC market (Triad, Charlotte or Triangle) publishes standalone assessment prices. All three NC markets show the same pattern: a free assessment, then a custom quote. The available NC rate data points (Charlotte $150-$199/hr; Charlotte managed IT $125-$250/user/month) sit in line with national figures.

### Cited Findings
- Charlotte: AT-NET Services lists a $1,000+ minimum project and $150-$199/hr average rates on Clutch (snippet) — [Clutch Charlotte MSPs](https://clutch.co/it-services/msp/charlotte). Network Essentials advertises a free IT assessment (snippet) — [G2 Network Essentials](https://www.g2.com/products/network-essentials/reviews). Petronella's 2026 Charlotte guide estimates managed IT at $125-$250 per user per month (snippet) — [Petronella Charlotte guide](https://petronellatech.com/blog/managed-it-services-charlotte-nc-how-to-choose-right-provider-what-to-expect-2026/).
- Raleigh: Petronella offers a "14-Day Risk-Free Managed IT Assessment" with custom pricing afterward (snippet) — [Petronella Raleigh](https://petronellatech.com/landing/managed-it-services-raleigh/). Triangle Tech Support plans are "typically $20K–$30K annually" (snippet) — [Triangle Tech Support](https://business.google.com/v/triangle-tech-support/010904578314175555167/7512/_/lp/2466552741821301422/).
- National guidance: MSPs in metro or high-cost areas charge more — [Kaseya MSP pricing](https://www.kaseya.com/resource/msp-pricing-managed-it-services-pricing/).

### Inferences
- The Triad is a smaller, lower-cost market than Charlotte or the Triangle. A reasonable expectation is that Triad buyers anchor at or below the national low tier, with $0 as the visible local competitor price. A published price of about **$1,000-$2,000 for one site** would sit below national paid norms ($1,500-$3,000 for under 25 endpoints) while still being a real fee. This is an inference, not measured data.

### Gaps
- I found no measured Triad vs Charlotte vs Triangle price differential for assessments or hourly IT rates. BLS wage data for network engineers by metro could stand in for it but was not gathered.
